# Instagram approved visual system（兼容历史路径）

> 三平台统一使用 [三平台统一成品视觉系统](../../textile-social-controller-v4/references/approved-visual-system.md)。本文件保留为兼容说明；若与共享规范冲突，以共享规范为准。

Apply this visual direction to Instagram production unless a later approved brief explicitly replaces it. It defines a boundary, not a template. Preserve the system while changing the furniture, room, camera position, palette, light direction, and type placement for each concept.

## Frame 1: product-centered editorial hero

- The product is the visual subject; a person is optional, not mandatory.
- Use a complete but controlled interior. Include supporting furniture or objects in foreground, middle ground, and background so the room does not feel empty or oversized.
- Use strong directional daylight, window shadows, reflected light, and practical lamps where appropriate. Shadows must follow one coherent physical light setup.
- Keep the material readable on the hero product. Supporting objects add scale and atmosphere without competing with it.
- Editorial typography is allowed: one short serif headline plus a restrained wide-tracked sans-serif descriptor works well. Place it in architectural negative space and keep it off the product.

## Frame 2: material evidence

- No people and no typography.
- Use a different camera angle and tighter scale than frame 1.
- Show evidence useful for judging the textile: texture unit, motif edge, pile direction, relief depth, sheen, seam, edge, drape, or compression.
- Keep the same material and product concept as frame 1, but do not reuse the same composition.

## Material fidelity gate

Compare the result directly with the source image before approval:

- retain the visible hue family and tonal variation;
- retain irregular motif outlines instead of regularizing them into dots or grids;
- retain the source texture-unit scale instead of enlarging small relief into large bobbles;
- retain pile direction, roots, sheen, and density when visible;
- keep texture scale believable on the chosen furniture and camera distance.

If any item fails, regenerate with a targeted correction. Do not approve an image solely because the room and lighting look premium.

## Typography gate

- Use only an approved product or material name and neutral visual words such as `TEXTURE`, `RELIEF`, `SCALE`, `DIRECTION`, or `SHADOW`.
- Do not invent performance, composition, certification, availability, price, or production claims.
- Do not imitate another brand's logo, lockup, or named product.
- Check spelling and letterforms at full resolution. No extra words or pseudo-letters are allowed.

## Variation gate

For a multi-concept test or recent production set, change all of the following where practical:

- product or furniture category;
- room function and architecture;
- dominant palette;
- camera position and subject placement;
- light direction and shadow pattern;
- typography scale and placement.

Five recolors of the same furniture or room do not count as five concepts.

## Hard exclusions

No copied account composition, visible third-party branding, logo, watermark, social UI, unsupported claim, impossible geometry, floating textile, empty AI showroom, random décor clutter, plastic-looking material, or misspelled text.

## Compatibility note

The V4 pipeline still uses the role and filename `human_context` / `human-context.png`. In Instagram production this now means the product-centered editorial hero described above; it no longer means that a human must appear.
